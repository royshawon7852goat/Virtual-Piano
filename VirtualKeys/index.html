VirtualKeys Keyboard Guide

Computer Keyboard Mapping (Active Octave Window)

The active octave window maps across two consecutive octaves (e.g. C4 to B5):

Lower Octave White Keys (C to B): A, S, D, F, G, H, J
Lower Octave Black Keys (C# to A#): W, E, T, Y, U
Upper Octave White Keys (C to B): K, L, ;, ', Z, X, C
Upper Octave Black Keys (C# to A#): O, P, ], 8, 9

Studio Controls

Sustain Pedal: Spacebar (Hold or Toggle)
Shift Octave Window Down / Up: Z / X
Start / Stop Recording: R
Play / Pause Song Playback: P
Metronome Toggle: M
Fit 88 Keys on Screen: F
